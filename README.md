# Alcapacit Services

Alcapacit Services has two divisions 

Alcapacit Service For Open, Domestic & Foreign Intelligence or ASODFINT

This division conducts investigations into corporations, movements and government activities happening in different countries and places worldwide as well as human intelligence and covert operations.

Alcapacit Service For Open Technology Systems or ASOTS

This division conducts developments of new public standards & protocols in different industries as well as other types of open source softwares.
